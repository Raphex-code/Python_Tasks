About Our Student Portal

A simple and organized platform designed to make student information easier to access, view, and manage.

Who We Are

Welcome to our Student Portal, a web-based application developed to help organize and manage student records.

The portal provides a central place to view student information, including names, identification numbers, email addresses, and ages.

This project demonstrates how web technologies and database management can work together to make information easier to organize and retrieve.

What You Can Do

Student Records

View registered students and access their basic information from one organized directory.

Organized Information

Keep student details structured in a database for easier retrieval and management.

Simple Interface

Navigate the portal through a clean, responsive interface on desktop and mobile devices.

Our Mission

Our goal is to demonstrate a practical, user-friendly approach to managing student information while applying the fundamentals of web development and database design.
